# Logo prompts for ChatGPT image generation

Structure follows OpenAI's image prompting guide: intended use, subject, details, constraints. Each prompt is the shared brief plus one concept line. Each asks for four variations in a 2x2 grid, so one run explores a concept instead of betting on a single render.

Concept letters match the sketches from the ideation session (A, B, C, F, G, H) plus a hybrid and an open brief.

## Shared brief

Paste this, then the concept line.

```
Design an original, non-infringing app icon for "PARIKSHANAM", an Indian olympiad-prep app for students in Class 6 to 10 (ages 11 to 16) and their parents. The name means "examination" in Sanskrit and Hindi. Intended use: Android launcher icon and Play Store listing, so the mark must read clearly at 48 pixels and survive as a single-colour silhouette. Style: flat vector, solid shapes, bold geometry, balanced negative space, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no text unless the concept says so. Palette: deep navy #1B3A6E, vivid orange #E8720C, warm off-white #F9F7F5, nothing else. Composition: one centred mark on a rounded-square tile with generous padding. Show 4 variations of the concept below in a 2x2 grid on a plain light grey background, square canvas.
```

## Concepts

**A. The right bubble**
```
Concept: an OMR answer sheet. Four answer bubbles in a 2x2 grid on a navy tile; three are empty off-white outlined circles, one is fully shaded solid orange as if filled in with a pencil, marking the right answer.
```

**B. Bubble p**
```
Concept: a lowercase letter p whose round bowl is a shaded OMR answer bubble: a solid orange circle with a navy outline ring, joined to a thick navy vertical stem with rounded ends. Off-white tile.
```

**C. Devanagari pa**
```
Concept: the Devanagari letter प (pa), the first letter of परीक्षणम्, drawn as a bold geometric glyph in off-white on a navy tile. Its top headline bar (shirorekha) is orange and extends slightly past the letter, like a bar the student clears. The letterform must be a correct, readable Devanagari प.
```

**F. Root tick**
```
Concept: a square-root sign that doubles as a check mark. The tick part of the radical is orange, the horizontal bar on top is off-white, on a navy tile. One confident continuous stroke with rounded ends.
```

**G. The flask**
```
Concept: a conical laboratory flask drawn in a bold navy outline, one third full of orange liquid, with two small orange bubbles rising. Off-white tile. It plays on परीक्षण meaning both an exam and a lab test.
```

**H. Arjuna's aim**
```
Concept: Arjuna's test from the Mahabharata, where he sees only the bird's eye. A stylised almond-shaped eye in off-white with an orange pupil, and a slim orange arrow entering from the lower left aimed at the pupil. Navy tile. Minimal and symbolic, no person and no literal bird.
```

**A+F. Rooted answer**
```
Concept: a square-root sign whose tick stroke is navy and whose top bar ends in a solid orange shaded answer bubble, as if an OMR answer is being marked. Off-white tile.
```

**Open brief**
```
Concept: propose 4 original concepts of your own, the kind a top Indian brand studio would present for this brief. Avoid books, graduation caps, light bulbs, rockets, owls and generic upward arrows. Label each variation with a one-word concept name under its tile.
```

## Round 2: Devanagari प only

Concept C won round 1. This round keeps the letter fixed and varies one thing per prompt. The brief now spells out the letter's anatomy, because "Devanagari pa" alone sometimes produces a shape that reads as Cyrillic ч.

```
Design an original, non-infringing app icon for "PARIKSHANAM", an Indian olympiad-prep app for students in Class 6 to 10 and their parents; the name means "examination". The mark is the Devanagari letter प (pa), the first letter of परीक्षणम्, with its headline bar (shirorekha) in orange. The letterform must stay a correct, readable Devanagari प: a headline bar across the top, a vertical stem on the right running down to the baseline, and a left stroke that comes down, curves at the bottom and joins the stem. Intended use: Android launcher icon and Play Store listing, readable at 48 pixels. Style: flat vector, solid shapes, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no text. Palette: deep navy #1B3A6E, vivid orange #E8720C, warm off-white #F9F7F5, nothing else. Show 4 variations in a 2x2 grid of rounded-square tiles on a plain light grey background, square canvas.
```

**R1. Rounded**
```
Variation brief: friendly and rounded. Build the glyph from thick strokes of even weight with fully rounded ends, in the spirit of a rounded geometric sans-serif like Nunito Black. Vary the tile: (1) navy tile, off-white glyph, orange bar; (2) off-white tile, navy glyph, orange bar; (3) orange tile, off-white glyph, navy bar; (4) navy tile, off-white glyph, orange bar extending past both sides of the letter.
```

**R2. What the bar means**
```
Variation brief: give the orange headline bar a meaning, one per tile: (1) the bar extends well to the right past the stem, like a raised bar the student clears; (2) the right end of the bar finishes in a solid orange circle, like a shaded OMR answer bubble; (3) the bar is a sharpened pencil lying flat, tip pointing right; (4) the bar rises slightly from left to right. Navy tiles, off-white glyph.
```

**R3. The bowl**
```
Variation brief: use the space inside the curve of the letter, one idea per tile: (1) a solid orange dot sits inside the bowl like a shaded OMR answer bubble; (2) the bottom curve of the bowl is drawn as a check mark; (3) the bowl is a clean negative-space shape, with a small gap where the left stroke meets the bar; (4) the bowl holds a small orange circle outlined in off-white. Navy tiles, off-white glyph, orange bar.
```

**R4. Modernist**
```
Variation brief: bold modernist construction, in the tradition of 1970s Indian institutional identity design. Build the letter only from rectangles, quarter circles and straight cuts, very heavy weight, tight counters. Four different constructions. Tiles may be navy, orange or off-white.
```

**R5. Certificate seal**
```
Variation brief: ignore the app-tile instruction. Make a circular seal for printed certificates and the splash screen. The प with its orange bar sits at the centre of a circle; vary the ring: (1) a single thick navy ring; (2) a ring of small evenly spaced circles like OMR bubbles; (3) a double ring; (4) a solid navy disc with the letter knocked out in off-white and the bar in orange. No text on the ring.
```

**R6. Small and single-colour**
```
Variation brief: stress-test the simplest version of the mark: (1) full colour on a navy tile, drawn as it would look at 48 pixels with no fine detail; (2) a single-colour off-white silhouette on an orange tile, for Android notification and themed icons; (3) single-colour navy on an off-white tile; (4) the bar and the letter merged into one solid shape with no gaps, navy on off-white.
```

## Round 3: Latin first, one memorable shape

Sandeep's point after round 2: a Devanagari mark reads as "Hindi medium" to a lot of Indian parents, whatever the name is. PhysicsWallah is the precedent, teaching in Hindi under a Latin PW. So this round leads with Latin shapes. The test for each one is whether a 12-year-old could draw it from memory after seeing it once.

Sandeep's own sketch is concept S. Its orange half and the shared stem form a प, and the stem and bowl form a Latin p, so it is a प|p ligature with the Devanagari tucked inside.

```
Design an original, non-infringing app icon for "Parikshanam", an Indian olympiad-prep app for students in Class 6 to 10 and their parents. The name means "examination". The icon must be as simple and memorable as the best tech logos: one bold, ownable silhouette that a 12-year-old could draw from memory, readable at 48 pixels and as a single-colour silhouette. Style: flat vector, solid shapes, bold geometry, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no words. No graduation caps, books, light bulbs, rockets or owls. Palette: deep navy #1B3A6E, vivid orange #E8720C, golden yellow #F5C842, warm off-white #F9F7F5, nothing else. Show 4 variations in a 2x2 grid of rounded-square tiles on a plain light grey background, square canvas.
```

**S. Sandeep's p (प|p ligature)**
```
Concept: a bold lowercase letter p whose stem is shared with an orange shape on its left. The orange shape is a top bar, a short left side and a rounded bottom that joins the stem, so the left half plus the stem reads as the Devanagari letter प and the stem plus the bowl reads as a Latin p. The counter of the p is filled solid golden yellow, like a shaded answer bubble. Vary: (1) navy p, orange left half, off-white tile; (2) the left half smaller and lighter so the Latin p leads; (3) navy tile, off-white p, orange left half; (4) one colour, navy on off-white, the two halves separated by a thin gap.
```

**B. Bubble p**
```
Concept: a lowercase p built from just two shapes, a thick vertical bar with rounded ends and a thick ring for the bowl. The inside of the ring is a solid circle, like an OMR answer bubble shaded with a pencil. Vary: (1) navy p, orange bubble, off-white tile; (2) off-white p, golden bubble, navy tile; (3) the bubble only half shaded, as if being filled in right now; (4) orange ring, navy stem.
```

**O. Answer-sheet P**
```
Concept: a capital P drawn on a grid of OMR answer bubbles. In a grid of small circles 3 columns wide and 5 rows tall, the filled circles form a bold letter P and the rest are empty outlines. Vary: (1) navy filled bubbles, pale outlined empties, off-white tile; (2) off-white filled bubbles on a navy tile; (3) no empty bubbles at all, only the filled ones; (4) a 4 by 5 grid with the last filled bubble orange, as if it was just marked.
```

**G. Geometry box P**
```
Concept: a capital P made from two instruments every Indian student has in a geometry box. The stem is a ruler and the bowl is a semicircular protractor attached to it, with the protractor's small centre notch forming the counter of the P. Bold solid shapes, at most four tick marks so it stays readable small. Vary the colours across the four tiles.
```

**M. Medal**
```
Concept: an olympiad medal reduced to its simplest form, where the medal disc and its ribbon together form a letter P or p. Four different ways of making the medal into the letter. Gold disc, navy and orange ribbon.
```

**1. Rank one**
```
Concept: a capital P whose stem is also the numeral 1, with the small flag of the 1 at the top of the stem, so the mark reads as both P and 1, meaning rank one. Four variations of how the flag and the bowl meet.
```

**Open brief, round 3**
```
Concept: propose 4 original concepts in the spirit of Apple, Nike, Target and Airbnb: one simple shape with one clever twist, about exams, olympiads, answers or the letter P. Label each variation with a one-word concept name under its tile.
```
